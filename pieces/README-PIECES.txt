KindleChess custom chess pieces
================================

Put custom transparent PNG files in:

  /mnt/us/extensions/kindlechess/pieces/custom/

Recommended image size:

  256 x 256 pixels
  PNG
  transparent background
  black/white/e-ink-friendly art
  square canvas

The app scales images to roughly 86% of each board square, so 256x256 is a good master size even when the board is smaller.

Required short filenames:

  White: wk.png wq.png wr.png wb.png wn.png wp.png
  Black: bk.png bq.png br.png bb.png bn.png bp.png

Long filenames are also accepted:

  white_king.png
  white_queen.png
  white_rook.png
  white_bishop.png
  white_knight.png
  white_pawn.png
  black_king.png
  black_queen.png
  black_rook.png
  black_bishop.png
  black_knight.png
  black_pawn.png

If a PNG is missing or invalid, KindleChess falls back to the built-in letter pieces for that piece.

After copying PNGs, open Settings in KindleChess and tap Reload PNGs, or exit and relaunch the app.
